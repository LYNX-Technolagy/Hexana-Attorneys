About the Firm

Led by Nwabisa Hexana

Nwabisa Hexana founded the firm in 2021 after more than sixteen years in legal practice. She is a former acting magistrate and has lectured at the University of Fort Hare.

That background matters. It means your matter is handled by someone who has seen the law from every side — as an attorney, as a magistrate, and as a teacher of the next generation of lawyers.

Hexana Attorneys is 100% black-owned and proudly based in East London. We believe good legal representation should be accessible, affordable, and honest.
